Privacy policy · 隐私政策

Local records and optional sharing

Shiftovia stores your roster, salary settings, actual work, family schedules and notes locally. OCR for imported images, documents and payslips runs on your device. We do not operate an advertising or analytics SDK.

If you invite someone with Apple iCloud, a private CloudKit share sends roster availability and explicitly shared family events to participants. Your private pay records, precise work locations and private notes are excluded. Manage the invitation to revoke access.

Devices, purchases and contact

Apple Watch receives a reduced roster and availability. You can separately choose to show earnings on the Watch or in iPhone/iPad earnings widgets. Optional Calendar access, notifications and alarms serve the actions you enable.

Apple processes StoreKit purchases and restoration. Version checks and recommendations query Apple’s App Store service. If you email support, we use your email address and the information you send to handle the request. To request deletion of a support message, email developer@typcb.cn.

Exports and deletion

You choose where exported calendars, reports and backups are saved. A full backup can contain private salary and family data. In More → Backup and export, Delete local journal removes the local journal. Manage shared invitations separately. Copies in external destinations are managed by the recipient.

本机数据与主动共享

班表、工资、实际工时和私人备注保存在设备上。排班照片、PDF 与工资单的文字识别在本机运行。应用未接入广告或分析 SDK。

通过 iCloud 邀请家人后，对方可以查看共享班表、忙碌时间和明确添加的家庭事项。私人薪资、精确工作地点及私人备注不在共享内容内。可以在邀请管理中撤销权限。

系统服务与删除

手表接收精简的班表和空闲时间。手表工资显示及工资小组件分别由你开启。日历、提醒和闹钟按你授权的操作使用。购买和恢复由 Apple 处理，版本和推荐应用信息从 Apple 获取。

导出与备份由你选择保存位置。可在“更多”的备份与导出中删除本机日记；共享邀请和已导出副本须分别管理。如通过邮件联系支持，我们会使用你提供的邮件信息处理请求。联系及删除支持邮件请求请发至 developer@typcb.cn。
