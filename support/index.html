Support · 使用支持

Create a roster

Choose a rotation and reference date, check the seven-day preview, then save. Use Calendar to edit a single occurrence. Add a family roster under Together to compare schedules.

Pay and purchases

Set the currency and hourly rate in Earnings. Pay figures are personal estimates based on your rules. Use Restore purchases with the same Apple Account to recover an eligible purchase.

Contact support

Email developer@typcb.cn with your app version, device model, and a description of the issue. Remove private pay or family details from any screenshots.

班表与家庭

选择轮班模板和参考日期，核对七天预览后保存。在“日历”编辑当天班次，在家人页面添加另一份班表后查看共同空闲。

工资、购买与联系

在工资页面设置费率和币种，预估结果请以劳动合同和实际工资单核对。用购买时的 Apple 账户恢复购买。遇到问题可发邮件至 developer@typcb.cn，说明机型、版本和操作步骤，截图请遮挡私人信息。
